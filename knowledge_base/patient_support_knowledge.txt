APPOINTMENT SUPPORT
Patients can request to schedule, reschedule, or cancel an appointment.
Appointment requests should include the preferred date, time, and type of visit.
Urgent medical concerns should not wait for a routine appointment and should be reviewed by the appropriate clinical team.


BILLING SUPPORT
Patients with questions about medical bills, charges, payment status, or payment options should be directed to the Billing department.
Billing staff can review account charges and explain available payment options.


INSURANCE SUPPORT
Questions about insurance coverage, claim status, or insurance information should be directed to the Insurance or Billing team.
Coverage and claim decisions depend on the patient's insurance provider and plan.


PRESCRIPTION SUPPORT
Prescription refill requests should be reviewed by the appropriate clinical team.
Patients should provide the medication name and relevant prescription information.
The system should not automatically approve medication requests.


MEDICAL RECORDS
Patients may request copies of their medical records through the appropriate medical records process.
Identity verification may be required before protected medical information is released.
